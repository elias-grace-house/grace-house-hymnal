NOT WHAT MY HANDS HAVE DONE

1
Not what my hands have done
Can save my guilty soul;
Not what my toiling flesh has paid
Can make my spirit whole.
Not what I feel or do
Can give me peace with God;
Not all my prayers,
And sighs and tears
Can bear my dreadful load.

2
Thy work alone, Oh Christ,
Can ease this weight of sin
Thy blood alone Oh Lamb of God,
Can give me peace within.
Your love to me Oh God,
Not mine, Oh Lord, to You
Can rid me of
This dark unrest,
And set my spirit free!

3
Your grace alone, Oh God,
To me can pardon speak;
Your power alone Oh Son of God,
Can this cruel bondage break.
No other work, save Yours,
No other blood will do,
No strength save that,
Which is divine,
Can bear me safely through.

4
I bless the Christ of God;
I rest on love divine;
And with unfaltering voice and heart,
I call this Savior mine.
His cross drives out each doubt,
I bury in His tomb
My unbelief,
And all my fear, 
Each lingering shade of gloom.

5
I praise the God of grace,
I trust His truth and might
He calls me His, I call Him mine,
My God, my joy, my light
It's He who has saved me
And freely pardon gives
I love because
He first loved me,
I live because He lives!
